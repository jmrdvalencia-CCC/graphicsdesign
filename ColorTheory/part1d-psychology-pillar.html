    🧠 Color psychology

    The emotional and behavioral responses that specific colors trigger in a viewer — the reason a red sale banner feels urgent and a blue bank logo feels trustworthy.

    What it covers

    How colors affect human behavior, emotion, and perception. In graphic design, understanding color psychology allows designers to align the emotional tone of a design with its intended message.

    Color psychology describes tendencies, not absolutes. Individual responses vary based on personal experience, context, and culture — but broad patterns are consistent enough to be reliable design tools.

    Why it mattersColor communicates before the viewer reads a single word. Research suggests color increases brand recognition by up to 80% and influences up to 85% of purchasing decisions.
