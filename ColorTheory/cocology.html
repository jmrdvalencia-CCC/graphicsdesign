🎨 The Color Journey

A Kokology Story Quiz — discover your true self through color

🚶 Scene 1: The Path of Colors

You wake up in a quiet, misty valley. Ahead, a long, winding road leads toward a glowing horizon. The sky is soft and warm. At your feet, four different paths stretch forward, each shining with a different color. Which one do you walk on?

🔴 Red Path
Bold, passionate, full of fire — you live in the moment and follow your heart.
🟠 Orange Path
Warm, joyful, creative — you bring sunshine wherever you go.
🟡 Yellow Path
Bright, hopeful, wise — you see the best in everything.
🟢 Green Path
Calm, steady, kind — you walk with peace and grow at your own pace.

🌅 Scene 2: The Sunset Sky

After walking for a while, you come to a clearing. Above you, a beautiful sunset paints the sky. Which color fills the horizon?

🔵 Deep Blue
Thoughtful, sincere, calm — you value trust and honesty above all.
🟣 Royal Purple
Magical, unique, dreamy — you see beauty where others don't.
🩷 Soft Pink
Gentle, loving, tender — you believe in kindness and forgiveness.
⚫ Twilight Black
Strong, mysterious, self-controlled — you protect your heart carefully.

🌊 Scene 3: The Crystal Lake

The sunset fades, and you stand beside a perfectly still lake. The water is clear — but deep down, you sense it holds a special color. What color do you see in the deepest part?

💎 Turquoise
Free-spirited, youthful, wise — you bring healing and clarity.
🩵 Sky Blue
Pure, open, peaceful — you carry light even on dark days.
🟤 Earth Brown
Warm, steady, loyal — your strength is quiet and deeply real.
⚪ Shimmering Silver
Graceful, reflective, bright — you shine with timeless elegance.

🏠 Scene 4: The House by the Shore

Night falls gently. Ahead, a cozy house stands waiting for you. Light glows from the windows. The door is open. What color is the house?

⚪ White House
You crave peace, clarity, and a fresh start — a place to simply be yourself.
🟡 Cream Yellow
You want warmth, laughter, and love — a home filled with happy memories.
🟤 Warm Brown
You want stability and belonging — a life that truly feels like home.
🔵 Blue House
You want calm, trust, and quiet happiness — something real and true.

🎁 Scene 5: The Waiting Gift

You step inside. On the table, a glowing gift box sits waiting — meant just for you. What color is the ribbon tied around it?

🧡 Gold Ribbon
Your destiny: to shine, inspire, and share your gifts with the world.
💜 Violet Ribbon
Your destiny: to create, heal, and bring magic wherever you go.
💚 Emerald Ribbon
Your destiny: to love deeply, grow, and build a life of peace.
❤️ Crimson Ribbon
Your destiny: to love bravely and live life fully — follow your heart.
✨ Open the gift inside… Your true color shines bright. You are perfect exactly as you are. ✨
