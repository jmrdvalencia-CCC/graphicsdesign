    Psychology & cultural meaning

    Color psychology describes tendencies, not absolutes — but the patterns are consistent enough to be reliable design tools. Color increases brand recognition by up to 80% and influences up to 85% of purchase decisions.

    Brands built on one color

    McDonald's — red + yellow → hunger + happiness Meta — blue → trust Spotify — green → growth, energy Tiffany & Co. — robin's egg blue → exclusivity IKEA — blue + yellow → Swedish trust + optimism Coca-Cola — red → energy, excitement, passion

    Color is not universal

    ColorWestern meaningElsewhere

    Design warningA palette that reads perfectly for a North American audience can send an unintended message to an Asian or Latin American one. Always research cultural color associations for international work.
