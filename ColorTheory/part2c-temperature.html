    Part 2 · Color Temperature

    Sort by temperature

    Colors carry a perceived warmth or coolness that affects how they feel spatially and emotionally. Tap a swatch to reveal its temperature.

    TemperatureColorsPsychological effect
    WarmRed, Orange, Yellow, Yellow-GreenEnergetic, urgent, passionate — feel closer (advancing)
    CoolBlue, Blue-Green, Violet, Blue-VioletCalm, trustworthy — feel farther away (receding)
    NeutralPure Green, Pure Red-Violet, Black, White, GreyBalanced, versatile — neither advancing nor receding
