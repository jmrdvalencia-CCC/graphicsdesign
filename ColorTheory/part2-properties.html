    The wheel & the four properties

    Every color a designer chooses can be described precisely along four axes. Master these and color stops being guesswork.

    Primary → secondary → tertiary

    Primary — red, yellow, blue (RYB) cannot be mixed from anything else.

    Secondary — red+yellow=orange, yellow+blue=green, blue+red=violet.

    Tertiary — one primary + one adjacent secondary.
