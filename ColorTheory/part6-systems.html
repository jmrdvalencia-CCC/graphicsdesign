    The six-step selection process

    1. Define the intent — emotion, audience, brand personality.
    2. Choose a base hue aligned with that intent.
    3. Choose a harmony — simple/elegant → monochromatic or analogous; high-energy → complementary or triadic.
    4. Adjust value & saturation for contrast and hierarchy.
    5. Apply 60-30-10, then test the layout in greyscale.
    6. Test accessibility — 4.5:1 contrast minimum, and color-blind safety (~8% of men have some deficiency).
