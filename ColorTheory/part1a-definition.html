    What is color theory?

    The practical guidance for mixing colors and predicting how combinations behave — the framework that explains why a palette works before a single word is read.

    Learning Objectives

    1. Define color theory and explain its foundational role in graphic design decision-making.
    2. Identify and describe the properties of color: hue, value, saturation, and temperature.
    3. Apply the color wheel to construct harmonious color schemes using complementary, analogous, triadic, split-complementary, and monochromatic relationships.
    4. Explain the psychological and cultural effects of color and apply them to design intent.
    5. Critique real-world designs for effective or ineffective use of color and propose improvements through group activity.
    Key conceptColor is never neutral. Every choice communicates mood, priority, brand identity, and cultural meaning. A designer who understands color theory makes intentional choices — one who does not makes accidental ones.
