    🎨 The color wheel

    The primary organizational tool of color theory — it arranges colors in a circular format to show their relationships and is the basis for all color harmonies.

    What it covers

    How colors relate to each other and how to build harmonious combinations from those relationships. The wheel organizes hues so that their connections — complementary, analogous, triadic — become immediately visible.

    Why it mattersEvery color harmony a designer uses — from a simple monochromatic palette to a complex tetradic scheme — is derived directly from the positions of colors on the wheel. Without the wheel, palette-building is guesswork.
