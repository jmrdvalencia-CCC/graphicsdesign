    The 60–30–10 rule

    Whichever harmony you pick, this proportion is the most reliable formula for applying it. Build your own below — drag a slider and the other two rebalance automatically.

    AreaColorProportion
    Page backgroundLight grey (neutral)60%
    Section headers & cardsDeep navy blue30%
    Call-to-action buttons, linksVivid orange10%
    Common mistakeUsing two complementary colors at equal saturation and equal proportion. Without a clear dominant, the pairing vibrates and reads as chaos rather than energy.
