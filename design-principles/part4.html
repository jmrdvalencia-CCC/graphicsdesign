Part 4 Summary & Assessment
4.1 — Key Takeaways

What We Learned Today

Core concepts from this module

  • 1
    Design Elements — Line, shape, color, texture, space, form, value, and typography are the building blocks of visual design.
  • 2
    Design Principles — Guidelines that govern how elements are arranged to create effective compositions.
  • 3
    Balance — The distribution of visual weight to create stability and harmony in a composition.
  • 4
    Three Types of Balance:
    SymmetricalMirror image · Formal · Stable
    AsymmetricalDifferent but equal weight · Dynamic · Modern
    RadialRadiates from center · Energetic · Focused
  • 5
    Visual Weight — Influenced by size, color, texture, position, and complexity of elements.
