# LOGO DESIGN ACTIVITY — CLIENT BRIEFS
## Graphics Design | 26–27 1st Semester
### Instructions: Choose one client brief and design a logo based on the information provided.

Each brief includes: industry, business type, mission, vision, culture, target audience, brand personality, mandatory elements, what it does, what it feels like, and what it values.

## 01. Verdant Roots Co.

**Target Audience:**

- Gender: All
- Lifestyle: Health-conscious, eco-aware, farmers market regulars
- Values: Sustainability, transparency, local economy

**Brand Personality:** Warm, trustworthy, grounded, unpretentious

**What It Feels Like:** Walking barefoot on grass. A handwritten recipe card. Sunday morning at the market.

**What It Values:** Honesty, soil health, community, simplicity

**Mandatory Elements:**

## ACTIVITY GUIDELINES

**Instructions:**
1. Choose **one** client brief from the list above.
2. Read all sections carefully before sketching — the brief is your contract.
3. Create a logo that reflects the brand's personality, values, and target audience.
4. Your logo must work in **full color** and **black & white**.
5. Submit your final logo as a `.PSD` file (layered) and a `.PNG` export (transparent background).

**Evaluation Criteria:**

| Criteria | Points |
|---|---|
| Relevance to the brief | 25 |
| Visual clarity and readability | 20 |
| Originality and creativity | 20 |
| Color palette adherence | 15 |
| Typography appropriateness | 10 |
| Black & white version works | 10 |
| **Total** | **100** |

> **Reminder:** A logo must work at the size of a business card and the size of a billboard. Test both before submitting.
