BEFORE WE BEGIN

AI Tools vs. Photoshop

Everyone's talking about AI image generators. So why learn Photoshop at all in 2026?

THE LANDSCAPE TODAY

What AI image tools do well

Tools like Midjourney, DALL·E, Firefly and Stable Diffusion generate images from text prompts in seconds.

✔ AI STRENGTH Rapid concept generation Turn a text prompt into a full image in under 10 seconds — great for mood boards and ideation.
✔ AI STRENGTH Style exploration Instantly try dozens of visual styles — oil painting, flat design, cinematic — without manual skill.
✔ AI STRENGTH Background generation Generate realistic or fantastical backgrounds on demand, no photography needed.
✔ AI STRENGTH Texture & pattern creation Produce seamless textures and complex patterns that would take hours to paint manually.
THE LANDSCAPE TODAY

Where AI still falls short

AI generates — it doesn't precisely edit. The moment you need pixel-level control, it hands the job back to Photoshop.

✘ AI WEAKNESS Exact retouching Removing a specific blemish, scar or object from a real photo requires manual precision — AI guesses.
✘ AI WEAKNESS Brand-accurate output AI can't reliably reproduce exact brand colors, logos or typography — Photoshop gives you full control.
✘ AI WEAKNESS Text rendering AI image generators still struggle with legible, correctly spelled text inside images.
✘ AI WEAKNESS Print-ready production CMYK color modes, bleed, resolution control and layer comps are Photoshop-only territory.
THE VERDICT

They're better together

The modern workflow isn't AI or Photoshop — it's AI then Photoshop. Professionals use AI to generate raw material, then Photoshop to refine, correct and deliver.

01

Generate a concept or background with Midjourney or Firefly.

02

Open the result in Photoshop for retouching, compositing and color correction.

03

Add brand-accurate text, logos and UI elements that AI can't reliably produce.

04

Export print-ready or screen-ready files with precise resolution and color settings.

💡 Knowing Photoshop makes you a better AI user — you understand what to fix and how.

Essential Tools

Seventeen tools every Photoshop user must know — from moving layers to masking, painting, and precision selection.

Use → or swipe to go through each tool

ESSENTIAL TOOL 01 / 17
V

Move Tool

The first tool in almost every session — repositions layers, objects and selections anywhere on the canvas.

  • Enable Auto‑Select to grab the layer you click on automatically
  • Hold Shift while dragging to lock to horizontal / vertical
  • Arrow keys nudge 1px · Shift+arrow nudges 10px
Click the layer, then drag — that's the whole workflow.
ESSENTIAL TOOL 02 / 17
M

Marquee Tools

Geometric selections — rectangle, ellipse, single row, single column. Right‑click the icon for all four.

  • Rectangular / Elliptical for crops and clean regional edits
  • Single Row / Column select one exact line of pixels
Hold Shift for a perfect square/circle · Alt to draw from the center.
ESSENTIAL TOOL 03 / 17
L

Lasso Tools

Freehand and semi‑automatic selection for irregular shapes.

  • Lasso — quick, rough freehand selections
  • Polygonal Lasso — straight‑edged anchor points, great for boxes and buildings
  • Magnetic Lasso — snaps to edges by contrast automatically
Use for cutouts, isolating subjects, complex color adjustments.
ESSENTIAL TOOL 04 / 17
C

Crop Tool

Trims, straightens and resizes the canvas — one of the most‑used tools in photo editing.

  • Set an exact aspect ratio (1:1, 16:9, 4:5) right in the Options Bar
  • Non‑destructive by default — cropped pixels hide, they don't vanish
  • Straighten auto‑corrects a tilted horizon
Press Enter to confirm · Esc to cancel.
ESSENTIAL TOOL 05 / 17
B

Brush Tool

Simulates natural media — paint, ink, chalk. Painting, sketching, retouching, masking, all in one tool.

  • Hardness — 100% sharp edge, 0% soft/feathered
  • Opacity vs Flow — transparency of the stroke vs. how fast paint builds up
[ / ] resize · Shift+[/] adjusts hardness.
BRUSH TOOL · SCENARIO

You're editing a portrait photo

A client sends you a headshot. The background is plain but the subject needs touch-ups — skin, hair, and a creative overlay. Every task below requires the Brush Tool.

Objective 1 — Soften skin — Paint on a new layer with a soft, low-opacity white brush over harsh skin tones to smooth them out.

Objective 2 — Mask the background — Add a Layer Mask, then paint black to hide and white to reveal edges around the hair.

Objective 3 — Add a light effect — Create a new layer set to Screen blend mode, pick a bright color and paint a soft glow over the subject.

Objective 4 — Paint a vignette — New layer set to Multiply, paint the edges with a large soft black brush to darken the corners.

Objective 5 — Sign the artwork — On a new layer, use a small hard brush to hand-write your name or add a decorative stroke.

ESSENTIAL TOOL 06 / 17
S
→

Clone Stamp Tool

Copies pixels from one area and paints them onto another — essential for restoration and retouching.

  • Hold Alt and click to set the source point
  • Release, then paint over the area you want covered
Use a soft, low‑opacity brush and re‑sample often to avoid repeating patterns.
ESSENTIAL TOOL 07 / 17
E

Eraser Tool

Removes pixels from a layer — to transparency, or to the background color.

  • Background Eraser — samples under the cursor, erases matching color
  • Magic Eraser — one click erases all similarly‑colored pixels
Better habit: use a Layer Mask instead — fully reversible.
ESSENTIAL TOOL 08 / 17
G

Gradient Tool

Fills a selection with a smooth transition between two or more colors.

  • Linear · Radial · Angle · Reflected · Diamond
  • Great for backgrounds, sky replacement, vignettes, overlays
The Paint Bucket shares this shortcut — right‑click to find it.
ESSENTIAL TOOL 09 / 17
P

Pen Tool

The most precise selection and drawing tool — builds vector paths from anchor points.

  • Click for corner (straight) anchors · click‑drag for smooth curves
  • Right‑click a finished path → Make Selection
Steep learning curve, cleanest cutouts — the industry standard.
ESSENTIAL TOOL 10 / 17
J
✦

Healing Brush Tool

Blends sampled pixels with the surrounding texture, light and shading — repairs vanish invisibly.

  • Spot Healing Brush — auto‑samples, no source point needed
  • Healing Brush — manual source point, more control
  • Also under J: Patch Tool · Red Eye Tool
Skin retouching, scratches, distracting elements — gone cleanly.
ESSENTIAL TOOL 11 / 17
T
Aa

Text (Type) Tool

Adds text as a vector layer — it stays sharp at any size until rasterized.

  • Horizontal / Vertical Type · Horizontal / Vertical Type Mask
  • Controls: font, size, color, alignment, tracking, leading, kerning
Double‑click a text layer thumbnail to jump back into edit mode.
ESSENTIAL TOOL 12 / 17
W
✦

Magic Wand Tool

Selects areas of similar color and tone with a single click.

  • Low Tolerance (≈10) — precise, narrow selection
  • High Tolerance (≈50–100) — broad, forgiving selection
Best for solid‑color backgrounds, flat‑color shapes, sky regions.
ESSENTIAL TOOL 13 / 17
I

Eyedropper Tool

Samples a color from anywhere on the canvas and sets it as the foreground color.

  • Also under I: Color Sampler · Ruler · Note Tool · Count Tool
  • Used to match brand colors and keep color consistent across layers
⚠ Common mix‑up: I is the Eyedropper — not the Magic Wand (that's W).
ESSENTIAL TOOL 14 / 17
O

Dodge & Burn Tools

Non‑destructive tonal adjustment directly on the image.

  • Dodge lightens · Burn darkens · Sponge shifts saturation
  • Range: Shadows / Midtones / Highlights, set in the Options Bar
Work on a duplicate layer, or dodge/burn on a grey overlay layer.
ESSENTIAL TOOL 15 / 17
J
⇄

Patch Tool

Lasso‑style selection + Healing Brush blending: drag a problem area onto a clean source.

  • Normal — standard patch replacement
  • Content‑Aware — synthesizes surrounding content for a natural result
Shares shortcut J — right‑click the Healing Brush icon to reach it.
ESSENTIAL TOOL 16 / 17
Y
↺

History Brush Tool

Paints back a previous state from the History Panel — a targeted, selective undo.

  • Check a state in Window → History, then paint over the area to revert
  • Art History Brush (also Y) applies states with painterly, stylized strokes
Great for restoring over‑edited areas without reverting the whole document.
ESSENTIAL TOOL 17 / 17
G

Paint Bucket Tool

Fills a selection, or a region of similar‑colored pixels, with a solid color or pattern.

  • Contiguous checked — fills only connected pixels; unchecked — fills all matches on the canvas
  • Fill with Pattern swaps solid color for a repeating pattern
Hidden under the Gradient Tool — right‑click to access it.

CRITICAL CONCEPT · PART 1

Photoshop is raster‑based — pixels, fixed at creation

Drag the slider to enlarge a raster image past its resolution. Photoshop has to invent pixels to fill the gaps — that's pixelation, and it can't be undone.

SUMMARY

Seven things to remember

01

Photoshop is raster‑based — it works in pixels, not vectors. 300 PPI for print, 72 PPI for screen.

02

Five interface zones: Menu Bar, Options Bar, Toolbar, Canvas, Right Side Panels.

03

The Options Bar is context‑sensitive — always check it after switching tools.

04

Memorize: V B C T P L M — the core shortcuts.

05

I is the Eyedropper, not the Magic Wand — the most common mix‑up.

06

Healing Brush (J) and Gradient (G) groups both hide extra tools in a right‑click flyout.

07

Non‑destructive editing — Layer Masks, Adjustment Layers, Smart Objects — is the professional standard.

BONUS · WHAT'S NEW 01

Smarter Selections

CS6 required manual lasso or pen work for complex cutouts. Modern Photoshop does it in one click.

NEW · W Object Selection Tool Draw a rough box — AI detects and snaps to the object inside it. Added CC 2020.
NEW · SELECT MENU Select Subject One click — AI isolates the main subject from the entire image automatically.
IMPROVED · SELECT MENU Select & Mask Workspace Replaced Refine Edge (CS6). Dedicated workspace for hair, fur and complex edges.
NEW · SELECT MENU Select Sky Instantly selects the sky region in any photo. Added CC 2021.
BONUS · WHAT'S NEW 02

Generative AI (Firefly)

Photoshop 2024–2025 integrates Adobe Firefly directly into the canvas — generate, extend and remove content with text prompts.

NEW · 2024 Generative Fill Make a selection, type a prompt — Firefly fills it with AI-generated content on a non-destructive layer.
NEW · 2024 Generative Expand Extend the canvas in any direction and AI fills the new space to match the existing image.
NEW · 2024 Remove Tool Paint over any object — AI removes it and reconstructs the background. No source point needed.
IMPROVED · 2023 Content-Aware Fill Workspace Dedicated workspace with sampling controls, far more powerful than CS6's basic Content-Aware Fill.
Generative layers are fully non-destructive — the original pixels are never touched.
BONUS · WHAT'S NEW 03
